## Public description

Planks on trestles run above black mud where the channel empties at low water. Forty stalls sell eel, salt, rope, and secondhand bronze fittings. The bell tower stands at the north end, and it has been silent for two days. People keep glancing at it and then keep working.

## Immediate danger

None standing. The planks are slick and a fall drops a character two metres into mud that holds a boot. The Magistrate constables here confiscate unregistered bronze on sight.

## Useful details

Every trader here knows Sera Vane by sight and knows she has not rung the evening bell. The fishmonger Rade sold her a smoked eel three nights ago and remembers she was carrying a lamp at midday, which is strange. The tide table nailed to the tower door shows tonight's high water two hours after sunset. The board is a single plank painted in a fishmonger's blunt hand, and its three lines read, in full: High water tonight, two hours after sunset. Low water at first light. No boat crosses the mud at the ebb.

## Hidden truths

A Choir Below listener sits in the drowned customs house across the mud and watches the tower with a glass. The market's bronze fittings include two pieces cut from a cracked bell, which is a capital offence and which four stallholders know about.

## NPC motives

Orso Pell walks the market twice a day counting barrels. He wants to know who is asking about the bell-keeper, and he asks pleasant questions to find out. He will offer the party paid work escorting a cargo, which is a test of their discretion.

## Discoverable clues

An active search of the tower door finds a lamp bracket with fresh oil, which places Sera Vane at the tower the night she vanished. Asking three traders about the bell-keeper produces the road shrine as her destination. A character who watches the customs house for ten minutes notices the glass reflecting.

## Consequences

If the party talks openly about the Choir, Orso Pell hears within the hour. If the party reports the cut bell bronze to the Magistrates, four stallholders are arrested and the market turns cold toward them for the rest of the session.
