## Public description

A dry vaulted chamber under the road shrine, reached by a stair behind the rear panel. Nine generations of bell-keepers lie in wall niches. At the centre, on a timber cradle, rests a bell of black bronze twice the size of the shrine bell. It has never been hung and it has never cracked.

## Immediate danger

The Brass Pilgrim stands in the dark at the far end and has stood there for an unknown time. It is a level 5 armoured figure that does not speak. It does not attack anyone who leaves the Black Bell on its cradle. It attacks immediately and without warning anyone who touches the cradle ropes.

## Useful details

Each niche carries a keeper's name and dates. The oldest niche holds a lead tablet, and the tablet is the original terms of the bargain, written in a script that predates the estuary towns.

The cradle ropes are sound. Four strong characters could raise the Black Bell to the shrine in about an hour of work.

## Hidden truths

The lead tablet says that the bells were never a wall. They were a clause. The sound is a periodic acknowledgement that the towns still hold to terms they no longer read, and the terms include an ending date that has passed.

Hanging the Black Bell renews the arrangement on the original terms, permanently, and the original terms require a keeper to go down. That is what Sera Vane worked out. That is why she went.

The Brass Pilgrim is the last keeper who went down, returned, and could not stop guarding the bell.

## NPC motives

The Brass Pilgrim wants the bell to stay on its cradle until someone reads the tablet. It has no way to say so. It will step aside, once, for a character who reads the tablet aloud in the crypt.

## Discoverable clues

An active search of the niches finds the tablet without a roll. Reading it requires an INT test only if the party is hurried or frightened; a character with the Forbidden knowledge or Bookworm background reads it outright.

## Consequences

Reading the tablet aloud stands the Brass Pilgrim down for the remainder of the session and unlocks Mother Keth's testimony. Touching the ropes without reading the tablet starts a fight the party is unlikely to win at level 1.
