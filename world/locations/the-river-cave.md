## Public description

A flooded cave mouth in the cliff below the road shrine, open only for the two hours around low water. Inside, a shelf of dry rock stands above standing salt water. The cave does not echo. A sound like a held bronze note comes from the water and does not stop.

## Immediate danger

The tide, absolutely. The mouth closes two hours after low water and does not open again for ten hours. Anyone inside when it closes stays inside. The narrator should state this clearly before the party enters, and should start a six-segment clock.

The thing in the water does not attack. It has no reason to.

## Useful details

Sera Vane sits on the dry shelf with a lamp, a slate, and two days of notes. She is exhausted, unhurt, and entirely lucid. She has been negotiating alone and she is losing, because she has no authority to promise anything and the water knows it.

Her notes record the terms as she understands them: the arrangement lapsed eleven years ago, the cracks are a consequence rather than a cause, and the water will accept either a renewed clause or an honest ending.

## Hidden truths

The water will accept a renewal only with a keeper, and it will accept an ending only if the towns are told the truth first. It considers being lied to the one intolerable term. It has kept every previous agreement exactly.

If the party promises anything on the estuary's behalf, the water treats the promise as binding and will hold them to it in later sessions.

## NPC motives

Sera Vane wants someone with standing, or someone reckless enough to substitute for standing, to finish what she started. She will not leave until the negotiation closes, and she will not let the party lie to the water.

## Discoverable clues

Talking with Sera Vane for ten minutes yields the whole shape of the situation. Her slate names the lead tablet in the crypt as the missing document. She does not know the Brass Pilgrim exists.

## Consequences

A promise made here becomes a campaign fact and should be recorded through `scene_commit` as a hidden fact and an open hook. Leaving Sera Vane in the cave past high water strands her for ten hours and advances the Choir's clock by two segments, because the Choir will reach her first.
