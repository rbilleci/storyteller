# Factions of the Black Estuary

Three powers matter in Vey. Each holds an alignment in the Law, Chaos, and Balance scheme. Each wants something specific and will trade for it.

## The Salt Magistrates

Alignment: Law. Roughly sixty clerks, forty armed constables, and three magistrates.

The Magistrates govern Vey through the bronze tax and the tide court. They want the bells repaired, the ledgers balanced, and every unregistered casting confiscated. They are not cruel by preference; they are cruel by procedure, because procedure is the only thing they have that works.

They will pay for information about unregistered bronze, and they will pay well for the return of a missing bell-keeper, because a missing bell-keeper is an unresolved entry.

Attitude toward the party: neutral, becoming useful if the party respects the tax and hostile if the party smuggles bronze.

## The Choir Below

Alignment: Chaos. Roughly forty adherents, unarmed, embedded in ordinary trades.

The Choir believes the bargain was theft and that the thing in the deep water is owed a hearing. They are not fanatics and they do not sacrifice people. They want the bells silenced long enough for terms to be spoken, and they are willing to let a season of salt into the fields to get it.

They recruit by argument, and their arguments are good. A player who talks with a Choir listener for an hour should come away genuinely uncertain.

Attitude toward the party: watchful. The Choir helps anyone who will carry a message and abandons anyone who hangs the Black Bell.

## The Ferrymen's Compact

Alignment: Balance. Roughly thirty pilots and their families.

The Compact owns the accurate charts and therefore owns the channel. They want the estuary navigable and the arrangement, whatever it is, stable. They distrust both the Magistrates' paperwork and the Choir's theology, and they have outlasted several of each.

They trade passage and charts for favours, never for coin from strangers.

Attitude toward the party: transactional, warming to loyal after one honoured debt.
